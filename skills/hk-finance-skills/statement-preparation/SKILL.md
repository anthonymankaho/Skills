# Statement Preparation (HKFRS/HKAS)

Prepare complete financial statements following Hong Kong Financial Reporting Standards.

## Framework

**Default HKFRS Format:**
1. **Statement of Financial Position** (Balance Sheet) — HKAS 1
2. **Statement of Comprehensive Income** (P&L) — HKAS 1
3. **Statement of Changes in Equity** — HKAS 1
4. **Statement of Cash Flows** — HKAS 7
5. **Notes to Financial Statements** (with accounting policies note first) — HKAS 1.112

## Steps

1. **Classify** each line item to HKFRS category (assets/liabilities/equity/revenue/expenses)
2. **Reconcile** trial balance to HKFRS presentation (ensure HKAS 2, 16, 37, etc. applied)
3. **Format** following HKFRS template order above
4. **Draft notes** — accounting policies, segment info (HKAS 8), related parties (HKAS 24)
5. **Review** — cross-footings, consistency, going concern (HKAS 1.25–26)

## HKFRS Specifics
- **HKFRS = IFRS** in substance; verify against latest HKICPA pronouncements
- **Going concern**: mandatory disclosure assessment at each reporting date (HKAS 1.25)
- **Related party disclosures**: mandatory even with HK private entities (HKAS 24)
- **HK Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622)**: additional disclosure requirements for incorporated companies

## Output
Complete draft financial statements ready for management review, with clear notes identifying where HKFRS judgment is required.
