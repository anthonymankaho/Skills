# Treasury Management

Optimize cash management, working capital, and liquidity for Hong Kong entities.

## Cash Forecasting

| Horizon | Frequency | Granularity |
|---------|-----------|-------------|
| 1-week | Daily | Transaction-level |
| 1-month | Weekly | Category-level |
| 3–12 months | Monthly | Scenario-based |

## Working Capital Optimization

| Metric | Formula | Target |
|--------|---------|--------|
| DSO | Avg Receivables ÷ (Revenue ÷ 365) | Industry benchmark |
| DIO | Avg Inventory ÷ (COGS ÷ 365) | Supply chain efficiency |
| DPO | Avg Payables ÷ (COGS ÷ 365) | Negotiated terms |
| CCC | DSO + DIO − DPO | Overall cycle speed |

## Hong Kong Treasury Considerations
- HKD-denominated liquidity primarily managed
- Banking relationships with HSBC, Hang Seng, DBS, UOB common
- MPF contribution deadlines and cash flow impact
- Quarterly dividend distribution cycles affect cash positions

## Output
Cash forecast model, working capital improvement plan, and banking relationship summary.
