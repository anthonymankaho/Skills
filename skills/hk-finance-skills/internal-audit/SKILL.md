# Internal Audit Processing (HKICPA Standards)

Full lifecycle internal audit aligned with Hong Kong Institute of CPAs standards.

## Four Phases

### Phase 1: Planning & Risk Assessment
1. Review previous audit findings and management responses
2. Understand entity's business model, processes, and control environment
3. Perform risk assessment → identify material misstatement areas
4. Develop annual/internal audit plan with resource allocation
5. Obtain management representation on scope and timeline

### Phase 2: Fieldwork & Testing
1. **Walkthrough tests**: trace transactions through key controls
2. **Design testing**: do controls exist and address identified risks?
3. **Operating effectiveness**: sample testing — do controls operate consistently?
4. **Substantive procedures**: detail testing on high-risk accounts
5. **Documentation**: workpapers with evidence, conclusions, and cross-references

### Phase 3: Reporting
Draft findings using **5 Cs Format**:
- **Condition** — what was found
- **Criteria** — what should have been (policy, regulation, best practice)
- **Cause** — root cause of the gap
- **Consequence** — actual or potential impact
- **Corrective Action** — recommended remediation

Severity Classification: Critical / High / Medium / Low

### Phase 4: Follow-Up & Monitoring
Track management action implementation status
Report progress to audit committee/board
Update risk-based audit plan based on new findings
Annual effectiveness self-assessment

## Hong Kong Compliance Requirements
- Independence checks per HKICPA Code of Professional Conduct
- Workpaper review (supervisory review per HKISAs)
- File completion checklist before archiving
- HK Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) internal audit expectations for listed entities

## Common Hong Kong Internal Audit Focus Areas
1. Related party transactions and disclosures (HKAS 24)
2. Going concern assessments (HKAS 1.25–26)
3. Revenue recognition complexity (HKFRS 15)
4. Lease accounting (HKFRS 16)
5. Impairment testing (HKAS 36)
6. Tax compliance (IRD s.14, 15A, 15AA)
7. Intercompany balances and elimination (HKFRS 10)
8. Cash flow forecasting and liquidity (HKAS 7)

## Output
Audit plan, workpapers, draft findings with 5Cs, management response log, final audit report, and follow-up tracker.
