# Journal Entry Preparation (HKFRS)

Prepare accurate journal entries compliant with Hong Kong accounting standards.

## Common JEs

| Scenario | Debit | Credit | HKFRS Basis |
|----------|-------|--------|-------------|
| Depreciation | Depreciation expense (P&L) | Accumulated depreciation (BS) | HKAS 16.58 |
| Prepaid expense | Prepaid asset (BS) | Bank/cash | HKAS 1.70 |
| Accrued revenue | Receivable (BS) | Revenue (P&L) | HKFRS 15.47 |
| Payroll/MPF | Salary expense | MPF payable + salaries payable | HK Companies Ordinance |
| Provision (Warranty) | Expense | Provision (HKAS 37) | HKAS 37.14 |
| Goodwill impairment | Impairment loss | Goodwill (contra) | HKAS 36.6 |
| HKFRS 16 lease | ROU asset / lease cost | Lease liability | HKFRS 16 |

## Checklist
- [ ] Purpose clearly stated
- [ ] HKFRS basis cited
- [ ] Amount reconciled to supporting document
- [ ] Account code verified in chart of accounts
- [ ] Approvals documented
