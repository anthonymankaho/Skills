# Risk Assessment

Identify, quantify, and manage financial risks using COSO ERM and ISO 31000 frameworks adapted for Hong Kong entities.

## Risk Categories

| Category | Examples | HK Context |
|----------|----------|------------|
| **Credit Risk** | Counterparty defaults, ECL (HKFRS 9) | HK banking regulatory alignment |
| **Market Risk** | FX exposure, interest rate sensitivity | HKD peg to USD, HKMA oversight |
| **Operational Risk** | Process failures, fraud, system outages | HKICPA internal control expectations |
| **Liquidity Risk** | Cash shortfalls, debt maturity mismatch | HK real estate lien positions |
| **Compliance Risk** | IRD penalties, SFC violations, HKICPA breaches | Multi-jurisdiction where applicable |

## Risk Assessment Process
1. **Identify** risks (brainstorm, process mapping, historical data review)
2. **Assess** likelihood (1–5 scale) and impact (1–5 scale)
3. **Plot** on risk matrix (heat map)
4. **Prioritize** top risks (likelihood × impact)
5. **Mitigate** — avoid, reduce, share, or accept each risk
6. **Monitor** and report to management/audit committee

## Output
Risk register, heat map, mitigation action plan, and risk report for audit committee.
