# Fixed Asset Accounting (HKAS 16 / HKAS 36 / HKAS 40)

## Capitalization Criteria (HKAS 16.7)
Recognize as asset when: (a) probable future economic benefits, AND (b) cost can be measured reliably. Include: purchase price, import duties, direct site assembly, initial estimate of decommissioning (HKAS 37).

## Depreciation (HKAS 16.58–63)
- Systematic allocation over useful life
- Residual value reviewed at each reporting date
- Methods: straight-line, diminishing balance, units of production
- HKIR 40 provides guidance on depreciation periods for common asset classes

## Revaluation Model (HKAS 16.31)
Option to use revaluation model instead of cost model. Requires regular valuations by qualified valuer. Surplus goes to OCI (revaluation surplus in equity); deficits go to P&L unless reversing prior surplus.

## Impairment (HKAS 36)
Compare carrying amount with recoverable amount (higher of fair value less costs to sell AND value in use). Impairment loss recognized in P&L; reversal permitted for assets other than goodwill.

## Disposals
Remove carrying amount and accumulated depreciation from BS. Gain/loss = proceeds − carrying amount — goes to P&L.

## Investment Property (HKAS 40)
Separate treatment from PPE. Can measure at cost or fair model. Fair gains/losses go to P&L. Distinguishing features: held for rental income or capital appreciation, not for use in production.

## Output
Fixed asset register, depreciation schedule, impairment test workbook, disposal journal proposals.
