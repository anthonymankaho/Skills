# Corporate Consolidation (HKFRS 10 / HKFRS 11)

## Control Assessment (HKFRS 10.B5–22)
Control exists when investor has: (a) power over investee, (b) exposure/rights to variable returns, AND (c) ability to use power to affect returns.

## Consolidation Steps
1. Identify subsidiaries (power test per HKFRS 10)
2. Align accounting policies across group
3. Combine like-for-like line items
4. Eliminate intercompany balances and transactions
5. Calculate NCI (non-controlling interest) share
6. Handle business combinations (HKFRS 3) and goodwill calculation

## Key Hong Kong Considerations
- **Two-tier profits tax**: only ONE group entity gets 8.25% preferential rate
- **Related party disclosures**: intra-group transactions still need attention (HKAS 24)
- **Foreign operations**: HKAS 21 translation reserves in OCI
- **HK Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622)**: consolidation requirements for holding companies

## Output
Consolidated worksheet, elimination schedule, NCI calculation, and consolidated financial statements draft.
