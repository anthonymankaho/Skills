# Working Capital Analysis

Deep analysis of the cash conversion cycle with optimization levers.

## Components

| Component | Driver | Optimization Levers |
|-----------|--------|-------------------|
| **DSO** (Days Sales Outstanding) | Collection speed | Credit policy, dunning process, early payment discounts, factoring |
| **DIO** (Days Inventory Outstanding) | Inventory turnover | JIT ordering, demand forecasting, obsolete stock write-downs |
| **DPO** (Days Payable Outstanding) | Payment timing | Negotiate longer terms, take advantage of discounts when beneficial |

## Cash Release Programs
1. **Receivables factoring** — immediate cash at discount cost
2. **Inventory optimization** — reduce slow-moving stock, consignment arrangements
3. **Payable extension** — renegotiate terms without damaging supplier relationships
4. **Advance collections** — request deposits from customers

## Hong Kong-Specific
- Typical HK trade terms: 30–60 days net
- Property developers: longer cycles (12–24 months construction)
- Manufacturing: moderate cycle (60–90 days typical)

## Output
Working capital diagnosis report, CCC trend analysis, and targeted improvement action plan with expected cash release per lever.
