# Tax Planning (Hong Kong IRD)

Optimize after-tax wealth under Hong Kong's territorial tax system while ensuring full IRD compliance.

## Constraints
- **AI cannot file tax returns** — this skill is for strategy and calculations
- All final filings must be prepared/reviewed by qualified CPA or tax advisor

## Optimization Framework

### Priority Order
1. **Compliance Verification** — All basic filings up to date with IRD
2. **Deduction Identification** — Capture all eligible business expenses (DIPN 4)
3. **Structural Optimization** — Is entity type most tax-efficient?
4. **Deferral Strategies** — Timing income/expenses to lower current liability

### Hong Kong-Specific Opportunities
- **Two-tier profits tax**: ensure single entity claim is optimized (8.25% first HK$2M)
- **R&D enhanced deduction**: 300% expense deduction from FY 2024/25
- **FSIE regime**: qualify foreign-sourced income for exemption from FY 2019/20
- **Capital allowances**: accelerate qualifying asset purchases (Schedule 4 IRO)
- **Loss carry-forward**: unutilized losses may be carried forward indefinitely (s.39A)

### Common Disallowable Items (DIPN 4)
- Entertainment expenses (limited deduction)
- Provisions (not actual losses)
- Capital expenditures (vs revenue)
- Fines and penalties
- Non-business-related expenses

## Output
Tax strategy memo with baseline liability, optimized liability, estimated savings, and action plan prioritized by implementation urgency.
