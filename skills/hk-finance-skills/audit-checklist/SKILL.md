# Audit Checklist (HKSAs Framework)

Generate structured audit programs aligned with Hong Kong Standards on Auditing.

## Standard Audit Procedures

| Area | HKSAs Basis | Typical Procedures |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| Revenue | HKSAs 330 | Vouch sales to shipping docs, confirm receivables, analytical review |
| Receivables | HKSAs 500 | External confirmations, aged trial balance, subsequent receipt testing |
| Inventory | HKSAs 501 | Observation, count, third-party confirmations, NRV testing |
| Fixed Assets | HKSAs 500 | Physical verification, title deeds, depreciation recalculation |
| Payables | HKSAs 500 | Supplier confirmations, subsequent payment testing, cutoff |
| Related Parties | HKSAs 550 | Inquiry of management, share register review, conflict checks |
| Going Concern | HKSAs 570 | Cash flow projection review, debt covenant analysis, board minutes |

## Workpaper Structure
Each workpaper includes: objective, reference to audit program, procedures performed, findings, conclusions, reviewer initials/date.

## Output
Customized audit checklist based on entity type, industry, and risk profile, with workpaper templates.
