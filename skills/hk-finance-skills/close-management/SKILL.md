# Close Management

Manage efficient period-end financial close process under Hong Kong standards.

## Close Calendar (Typical HK Entity)

| Day | Activity |
|-----|----------|
| Day 1–2 | Subledger cut-off, accruals estimation |
| Day 3–5 | Fixed asset roll-forward, depreciation run |
| Day 4–6 | Bank reconciliation, cash confirmation |
| Day 5–7 | Intercompany matching & eliminations |
| Day 6–8 | Revenue review (HKFRS 15 milestone check) |
| Day 7–9 | Trial balance preparation & variance analysis |
| Day 10 | Draft financial statements, management review |

## Fast Close Techniques
- Pre-close during month-end (parallel processing)
- Automation of bank recs and intercompany matching
- Rolling close for recurring JEs
- Clear SLA between finance, ops, and external auditors

## Output
Close calendar, task assignment tracker, and post-close retrospective with cycle time metrics.
